About

Full pampering… with a feminine, luxe touch

At ZOEYA Beauty Lounge we offer a complete beauty experience in a calm, elegant setting. From nails and hair to skin, brows and lashes, every detail is built around you so you can take the time you deserve — with special care for bridal packages.

  • Nails: manicure, pedicure and nail art (Nail Art & French)
  • Hair: coloring & highlights, blow-dry and styling
  • Skin & face: cleansing, care and masks
  • Brows & lashes: shaping and definition
  • Bridal packages: a complete look for your big day
